# Using the collection for discussion

These are educationally themed games, not validated curricula or assessed learning interventions. Review a game before using it with a class; check age suitability, controls, accessibility, and available time rather than assuming these are uniform across the collection.

## A simple session

1. Choose a question before playing: what trade-off will you investigate?
2. Play a short scenario and record a consequential decision without collecting personal data.
3. Compare two possible approaches. Which consequences come from the simulation's rules?
4. Consult an appropriate historical or scientific source. Where does the game simplify uncertainty?
5. Explain what the game helped you notice and what it cannot establish.

## Reflection prompts

- **History and civics:** Whose perspective is represented? What constraints shape leadership? What evidence would challenge the game's interpretation?
- **Nature:** Which ecological conditions matter? How does a model differ from real environmental observations and current local regulations?
- **Space and science:** Which resource bottlenecks appear? Which assumptions would require engineering measurements before a real mission?
- **Medical reasoning:** What information is missing? How can uncertainty be expressed? Do not use gameplay to diagnose anyone or substitute for professional training.

Session durations and learning gains have not been measured for this catalog. Save handling varies by game. Do not upload student records, real medical cases, identifying screenshots, or private browser-save data to public issue trackers.
